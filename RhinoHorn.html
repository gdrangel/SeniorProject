Rhino Horn 3-80S

Rhino Horn

A 4-sided blade designed to be compact and dense. It is unique among blades for being noticeably smaller in diameter than any other. Despite this, its weight is still comparable to them, indicating a greater density of metal. The smaller area also means that more weight is concentrated on the central axis, preventing it from being pushed around by opponents. It also features a semi-aggressive shape, generating a great amount of recoil when coming into contact with opponents. These features are meant to give it the ability to "counterattack" opponents when hit, forcefully pushing both Beyblades away on contact.

3-80

A 3 sided ratchet which measures to 8 millimeters.

S

A passive bit with low burst resistance. The sharp surface creates instability, causing the Beyblade to tilt after losing balance. It has a smaller point than N, and thus can be tipped over much more easily.
