Leon Claw 5-60P

Leon Claw

A 5-sided blade designed to have a starkly different performance depending on the movement of the customization. The design features an aggressive upper side and a defensive lower side. When the blade is moving aggressively, opponents are exposed to the offensive upper side, where 5 jagged, claw-like blades deliver attacks. When the blade is moving passively, opponents are exposed to the defensive lower side, where attacks are deflected and ignored. When used with parts that change performance, both aspects can be utilized during battle.

5-60

A 5-sided ratchet which measures to 6 millimeters.

P

A semi-aggressive bit with high burst resistance. The bit features two surfaces, a Ball-like segment in the center alongside a Flat-like segment surrounding it. The performance of the bit is dependent on the angle at which it was launched.
