GB (Gear Ball)

Analysis

A semi-passive bit with low burst resistance. The round surface creates stability, causing the Beyblade to move around casually while conserving stamina. Unlike the standard B, GB's gear design extends down to the bottom of the bit, creating a more forceful dash when locked onto the extreme line.

Value

As a stamina-oriented bit, GB experiences issues from the extended gear that cause it to burst itself upon attacking from the extreme line. As a result, GB is considered one of the worst bits in the game.
