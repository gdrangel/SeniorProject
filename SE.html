Shark Edge

Analysis

A 2-sided blade with a unique design to facilitate upper attacks. The blade possesses solid points for smashing attacks, but its real value is in the ability to perform upper attacks from below the opponent. Each side of the blade is sloped, creating a ramp for reaching underneath opponents and "scooping" them up. These sloped blades give it an improved ability to launch opponents out of the stadium, or otherwise making them burst by targeting the ratchet.

Value

Shark Edge is one of the most valuable blades in the game. The ability to attack opponents from below is incredibly useful, to the extent that many combinations choose to use lower ratchets just to minimize risk against it. With weight comparable to Dran Sword, there exists little reason to not consider using Shark Edge customizations. As such, it is a must-have part for any competitive player.
