Wizard Arrow

Wizard Arrow

A 2-sided blade designed to maximize stamina and stability. The first stamina-oriented blade in Beyblade X, it is the standard that all other stamina types are modelled after. The blade features 2 smooth sides that minimize wind resistance in order to conserve stamina as much as possible. The weight on each side is distributed as far from the center as possible, generating strong centrifugal force when spinning. The weight within the blade is also distributed evenly, preventing any destabilization from opponents. These attributes cause the blade to excel at prologing a customization's spin time as much as possible.
