Exposition

Hello! This website is designed to be a comprehensive database of everything pertaining to Beyblade X, the most recent line of japanese company Takara Tomy's hit hobby/toy franchise, Beyblade.

I have developed this website to fill the desire of competitive Beyblade players to have a central source of information for the products.

This website is split into various sections related to different aspects of the line, such as the Beyblades, the products they are available from, and the individual parts which make up said Beyblades.

As my senior project, my aim is that the design and functions of this website can adequately convey to you the various concepts I have learned and practiced over the course of obtaining my degree. Enjoy!
