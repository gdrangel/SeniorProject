Unicorn Sting

Analysis

A 2-sided blade in which both sides are drastically different from each other. The blade features 2 distinct sides with completely different shapes, creating an imbalance. One side possesses jagged edges, delivering strong attacks to opponents. The other side, meanwhile, is smooth, deflecting attacks from opponents. These sides enable the blade to both attack and defend, assuming the right side makes contact when it needs to.

Value

While one might assume that the inability to determine what side makes contact at what time may be a disadvantage, both sides actually perform just as well as blades designed for each. Unicorn Sting sees solid usage in competitive settings, being able to utilize both sides to efficiently respond to most opponents thrown at it. As a result, Unicorn sting is a medium-priority part holding a valid niche.
