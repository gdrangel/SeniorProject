Knight Shield 3-80N

Kight Shield

A 3-sided blade designed to block attacks from any angle. The first defense-oriented blade in Beyblade X, it is the standard that all other defense types are modelled after. The blade features 3 sides which form a round, thick wall around the blade. The weight on each side is distributed as close to the center as possible, creating a strong central axis that prevents it from being pushed around by attacks. With no gaps in it's perimeter, it lacks a weak point for opponents to strike best. However, the blade is imbalanced, causing it to quickly wobble and lose stability.

3-80

A 3 sided ratchet which measures to 8 millimeters.

N

A standard, passive bit with low burst resistance. The sharp surface creates instability, causing the Beyblade to tilt after losing balance.
