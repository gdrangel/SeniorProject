HN (High Needle)

Analysis

A passive bit with low burst resistance. It measures 1 millimeter taller than standard bits. The sharp surface creates instability, causing the Beyblade to tilt after losing balance. Unlike the standard N, HN features a wider angle to allow for lower tilting than N.

Value

As defense-oriented bit, HN can be utilized on almost any defense customization you want, and is generally more useful than N.
