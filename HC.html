Hells Chain

Analysis

A 5-sided blade whose performance changed based on the scenario. An alternative to Hells Scythe, this blade's abilities are different depending on the scenario. The upper side of the blade is designed offensively, to be used against taller defensive opponents. Conversely, the lower side of the blade is defensive, for use against shorter aggressive opponents. The blade is best used on medium-height combinations so that it can make full use of both sides.

Value

Hells Chain, as a balance-oriented blade, is designed to have varying degrees of usefulness against different opponents. However, in a competitive setting, its best use is for its defensive properties. The lower side performs exactly as intended, and is a solid choice for deflecting attacks from lower opponents. As such, Hells Chain is just medium-priority, a solid choice for its use.
