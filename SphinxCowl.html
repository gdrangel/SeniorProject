Sphinx Cowl 9-80GN

Sphinx Cowl

A blade with an ambiguous number of sides designed for impact dispersal. Unlike most blades, which are designed to be symmetrical, this blade features a mirrored design, creating a weight imbalance. The main aspect of this blade are the numerous bumps around it. When taking an attack, the bumps are meant to help disperse the force of the impact across the full area, lessening the effect.

9-80

A 9-sided ratchet that measures to 8 millimeters.

GN

A passive bit with low burst resistance. The sharp surface creates instability, causing the Beyblade to tilt after losing balance. Unlike the standard N, GN's gear design extends down to the bottom of the bit, creating a more forceful dash when locked onto the extreme line.
