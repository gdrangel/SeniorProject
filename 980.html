9-80

Analysis

A 9-sided ratchet that measures to 8 millimeters.

Value

As 9-sided ratchets possess a thinner ring of contact points than other ratchets, 9-80 finds use in tall, defense-oriented customizations, to reduce the risk of opponents hitting the ratchet.
