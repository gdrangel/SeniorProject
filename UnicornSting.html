Unicorn Sting 5-60GP

Unicorn Sting

A 2-sided blade in which both sides are drastically different from each other. The blade features 2 distinct sides with completely different shapes, creating an imbalance. One side possesses jagged edges, delivering strong attacks to opponents. The other side, meanwhile, is smooth, deflecting attacks from opponents. These sides enable the blade to both attack and defend, assuming the right side makes contact when it needs to.

5-60

A 5-sided ratchet which measures to 6 millimeters.

GP

A semi-aggressive bit with high burst resistance. The bit features two surfaces, a Ball-like segment in the center alongside a Flat-like segment surrounding it. The performance of the bit is dependent on the angle at which it was launched. Unlike the standard P, GP's gear design extends down to the bottom of the bit, creating a more forceful dash when locked onto the extreme line.
