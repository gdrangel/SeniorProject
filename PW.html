Phoenix Wing

Analysis

A 3-sided blade designed to strike hard with its weight. At 38 grams, it rivals Cobalt Drake in weight. Similarly, its 3 sides each possess a notable, distinct contact point that delivers an exceptionally powerful attack against opponents. These together make it a powerful blade for any customization. However, its power is so great that the distinct points can become blunted and flattened with use, meaning it should be used with caution.

Value

Similarly to Cobalt Drake, Phoenix Wing is considered to be one of the best blades in the game. It is significantly more accessible than Cobalt Drake, making it a first choice for many players looking for a strong attacker. Plus, it also performs well in other types of customizations, such as balance types, meaning it has a versatility unlike any other blade. As such, it is a high-priority blade that anyone should own multiple of.
