Knight Lance

Analysis

A 3-sided blade designed to counter from above. An alternative to Knight Shield, this blade features contact points set up higher than usual. Its semi-aggressive contact points are designed to display their full potential when the blade is tilted down. By striking down from above, this blade can firmly thrust opponents towards the ground. This makes it especially effective at knocking opponents off balance. The smoother, less aggressive segments between the gaps are also designed to make use of tilting, shielding against attacks from lower opponents.

Value

Knight Lance is a solid choice for many customizations. By making use of tilting, Knight Lance works best on tall ratchets and bits, inviting opportunities for unique and innovative combinations. It can also be used on lower, aggressive parts as a solid upper attacker. As a whole, Knight Lance is a medium-priority part, a solid addition to any player's assets.
