Viper Tail 5-80O

Viper Tail

A 6-sided blade designed to passively wear down the opponent. The blade features an unconventional set of 6 downward-facing points. These points are meant to shave the stamina of opponents over time with repeated, continual attacks. Conversely, the rest of the blade is relatively smooth, in order to minimize wind resistance. These aspects combined create a blade with the ability to damage the opponent with minimal recoil.

5-80

A 5-sided ratchet which measures to 8 millimeters.

O

A semi-passive bit with low burst resistance. The round surface creates stability, causing the Beyblade to move around casually while conserving stamina. It has a smaller surface area than B, and as such it has the capacity to spin for longer.
