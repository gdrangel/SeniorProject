Hells Scythe 4-60T

Hells Scythe

A 4-sided blade designed to reach a balance between offensive and defensive qualities. The first balance-oriented blade in Beyblade X, it is the standard that all other balance types are modelled after. The upper side of its 4 blades is styled to be jagged for attacking opponents, while the lower side is smoother for deflecting opposing attacks. The shape is also designed to reduce air resistance, improving stamina. These qualities together give the blade a wide range of uses depending on the parts it is paired with.

4-60

A 4-sided ratchet which measures to 6 millimeters.

T

A standard, semi-aggressive bit with high burst resistance. The semi-flat surface creates friction against the stadium, causing the Beyblade to rush around without expending too much stamina.
