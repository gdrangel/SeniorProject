Dran Sword 3-60F

Dran Sword

A 3-sided blade designed for strong attacks. The first attack-oriented blade released in Beyblade X, it is the standard that all other attack types are modelled after. Its 3 blades posess jagged edges that help deliver solid smashing attacks against any opponent. Weighing an average of 34-35 grams, it stands at a medium weight that enables it to remain effective against heavier opponents with the right parts. The simple yet effective shape lends itself to a wide variety of uses in different kinds of attack customizations. Over time, the jagged edges will wear down with use, resulting in a smoother shape more suited to stamina customizations.

3-60

A 3-sided ratchet which measures to 6 millimeters.

F

A standard, aggressive bit with high burst resistance. The flat surface creates friction against the stadium, causing the Beyblade to rush around aggressively.
