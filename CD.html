Cobalt Drake

Analysis

A 4-sided blade with an exceptional weight. This blade was released through a special promotional campaign, challenging users to engage in an online raffle with points earned from purchasing other products in order to obtain. As a result, this blade was designed to be especially powerful compared to others, weighing in at a steep 38 grams. The 4 jagged sides make use of this weight to deliver smashing attacks much greater than most other blades can produce. With notable weight and power together, this blade is very valuable.

Value

Cobalt Drake is often considered one of the best blades in the game. It goes without saying that combinations utilizing this blade are heavily desired in competitions. Unfortunately, this means that Cobalt Drake has a high barrier of entry to use, as this blade can only be obtained from those who won the promotional campaign. Selling for hundreds of dollars online, it is too difficult for many players to be able to use. Cobalt Drake is a high-priority part, but should only be considered if one is prepared to make the necessary investment.
