HT (High Taper)

Analysis

A semi-aggressive bit with high burst resistance. It measures 1 millimeter taller than standard bits. The semi-flat surface creates friction against the stadium, causing the Beyblade to rush around without expending too much stamina. Unlike the standard T, HT features a slightly wider angled tip.

Value

As a balance-oriented bit, HT can be utilized on a variety of different combinations depending on your needs.
