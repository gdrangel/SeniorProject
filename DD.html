Dran Dagger

Analysis

A 6-sided blade designed to produce constant, continuous attacks. An alternative to Dran Sword, this blade focuses less on strong smash attacks, opting for smaller barrage attacks instead. By utilizing a design with less extreme contact points, this blade is able to attack repeatedly without consuming much stamina. This results in greater stability and longevity, which are qualities not often seen in attack-oriented blades.

Value

Dran Dagger is a blade with just as much potential as Dran Sword. Although it sees less use than Dran Sword, it is effective at wearing down an opponent, and can be just as successful in the right hands. As a result, Dran Dagger is neither high-priority or low-priority, best for those with a distinct strategy in mind for it.
